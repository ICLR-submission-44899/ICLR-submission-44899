Leave-task-out retrieval over 882 episode-disjoint queries and 3,618 gallery transitions, top-5 neighbors. Jaccard@5 measures how often Wan-VAE and DINOv3 retrieve the same neighbors; the behavioral metrics compare each query with its neighbors, averaged over both backbones.

Retrieval protocol

Neighbors are selected by cosine distance among 3,618 gallery transitions, excluding the query's task. The native readout spatially averages each frame's features; PRISM uses its full-grid posterior mean (3 × 4 × 4 tokens, 64 dimensions). Both see the observed future, so this tests representation geometry, not policy prediction. The four examples are illustrations.

Figure S3PredictionsPredicted vs. recorded video

The predictions are visually faithful, yet this policy is clearly weaker than PRISM on LIBERO-Plus-2K (48.5% vs. 58.1%, averaged over two seeds). Native co-training is therefore not limited by failing to predict the future.
